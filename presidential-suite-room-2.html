Presidential Suite for Staycation & Daycation in Delhi NCR

Stay in absolute luxury with your family or friends in one of our family suites that offers two attached bedrooms with a common bathroom.

The resort offers a grand Presidential Suite, a spacious room with a master bedroom including a bed, separate sofa seating, a workstation desk, a dining table and a safe; apart from all the modern provisions of a room. It extends to another room for exclusive and lavish living room that holds a personal lounge seating for a large group. It is apt for extended groups to gather in a private common room for ceremonies, games, chit-chats and more. The room has a unique identity in terms of warm interiors, ecstatic view of lawns, comfortable infrastructure and pleasant utilities. Huge glass window overlooking the plush green grounds add to the magnificent view of the room. Various options of seating outside the rooms make it a pleasant open-air experience and unblocked fun space for children to enjoy.

Guest facilities include Open Gym, Badminton court, open lawns for free play, morning tea stall, and more. All these facilities make AapnoGhar the best resort for family holiday near Delhi and Gurgaon and the perfect venue for family stay in Gurgaon.

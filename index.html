Need to Know

BEFORE YOU VISIT: POLICIES & FAQS

Key details regarding park timings, swimwear guidelines, height rules, and entry policies.

⏰ What are the park timings and operating days? +
AapnoGhar Water and Amusement Parks are open 365 days a year from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM. The Baza Restaurant serves continuous meals and snacks throughout the operating hours.
👙 Is synthetic/lycra swimwear mandatory for the water park? +
Yes, for hygiene and safety reasons on high-speed slides, standard nylon or lycra swimwear is mandatory. Costumes and towels are also available for purchase and rent at our on-site changing counters.
🚫 What is the policy regarding stag/solo entries? +
To ensure a safe, peaceful, and family-friendly atmosphere, stag/single male entries are not permitted in the water and amusement parks unless accompanying families or as part of a registered corporate group.
📏 What are the height restrictions and child ticketing policies? +
Children below 33 inches are complimentary. Children between 33 inches and 4.5 feet require a Kid Pass. Guests above 4.5 feet are ticketed as Adults.
🚗 Is parking available at the venue? +
Yes, AapnoGhar offers spacious, secure on-site parking for over 500+ four-wheelers and two-wheelers, completely free of cost for all guests.

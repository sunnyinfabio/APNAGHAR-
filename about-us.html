Airport Motel And Baza Restaurant (AapnoGhar Resort) offers myriad possibilities for relaxation, entertainment and recreation with an ethno-modern luxury resort within Delhi/NCR since 1994. Located conveniently on Delhi-Jaipur Expressway on National Highway 8, Gurugram, the 9-acre wonderland exists in harmony with the magic of nature, being a one-stop recreation centre around Delhi/NCR. The promoters are dynamic businessmen in the hospitality industry from the last 35 years.

Among the resorts in Gurugram, AapnoGhar is reckoned as one of the best entertaining places, with a lush green amusement park, thrilling water park, cheering food, relaxing rooms, party lawns, banquet halls and more, all under one roof.

AapnoGhar’s 67 well-appointed rooms are spacious, tastefully designed & have everything to make one’s stay comfortable in Delhi/NCR.

The multi-cuisine Baza Restaurant offers North Indian, South Indian, Chinese and Continental food to cater to the Indian Palate.

The Amusement park has 21 thrilling rides and 24 adventure activities for all age groups (a special area of rides has been created for smaller children) with a variety of attractions like musical stage programs, puppet show, activity centre etc.

For a water-packed adventure in AapnoGhar Water park, slip and slide your way down the twists and turns of Turbo Twister, Family Fun slide and Aqua Tube slide spiraling down at incredible speed. Kids’ attractions include multi activity play pans, a giant tipping bucket, shallow water lagoons, rain huts, shower pillar and more. A vast leisure pool with water depths to suit all ages with family slides and the rain dance pool awaits all guests.AapnoGhar presents two lush green party lawns, a banquet hall with ad joining open space and private party hall. It is an ideal place for hosting marriage functions, corporate get togethers, conference/conventions, birthdays, kitty parties and picnics. We offer customize packages suitable to your needs.
